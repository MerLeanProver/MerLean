## 3a. Main results (from the critical path)

Two results, one through-line:

- **R1 (Theorem, = S18).** For every integer n ≥ 0 and every A ∈ M_n(ℝ) with A² = A:
  tr A = rank A; in particular tr A is a nonnegative integer with 0 ≤ tr A ≤ n.
- **R2 (Proposition, = S16, the characteristic-free form).** For every field F and every
  idempotent A ∈ M_n(F): tr A = (rank A)·1_F. (The ledger proves it for ℝ but rows
  S0–S16 consume no property of ℝ beyond "field"; the paper states R2 with that scope
  and proves it by the same argument, since the ledger's own audit — verified twice by
  the referee — is that char 0 enters only at S17.)

Through-line (one sentence): a rank factorization turns idempotence into the identity
CB = I_r, making trace-equals-rank a one-line trace computation whose only
characteristic-zero content is the final reading of r·1_F as the integer r.
